# Data availability

Every dataset used in this study is openly available.

| Layer | Product | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Study-area boundary | GADM level-2 administrative units | https://gadm.org/ |
| Elevation, slope, aspect | NASADEM 30 m digital elevation model | https://earthengine.google.com/ |
| Mean annual temperature | WorldClim 2.1, BIO1 | https://www.worldclim.org/ |
| Annual precipitation | WorldClim 2.1, BIO12 | https://www.worldclim.org/ |
| Land use / land cover | ESA WorldCover 2021 v200, 10 m | https://esa-worldcover.org/ |
| Distance to roads | OpenStreetMap road network | https://www.openstreetmap.org/ · https://download.geofabrik.de/ |
| Distance to freshwater | JRC Global Surface Water, maximum extent | https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/ |
| Distance to settlements | JRC Global Human Settlement built-up surface | https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu/ |

Slope and aspect are derived from the elevation surface. The three distance
surfaces are Euclidean distances computed on the common study-area grid.

## Derived layers

The criterion rasters, the composite suitability surface and the classified
output are reproducible from the scripts in this repository together with the
parameters published in the article, and are not distributed here.

## Occurrence records

The 165 georeferenced occurrence records were compiled from the published
herpetological literature cited in the article and supplemented by field
observations verified by the authors. *Mertensiella caucasica* is assessed as
Vulnerable, so precise coordinates are withheld to reduce collection risk and
are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.
